# 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — Official Brief
**Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Subjects:** Human Exploration, Software, Space Exploration

## Summary
Long-duration missions expose astronauts to space radiation, isolation and confinement, altered gravity, and a hostile closed environment, which can bring immune changes, bone loss, cardiovascular events, and behavioral health problems. On long missions, astronauts carry much of the responsibility for spotting these changes in themselves. Your challenge is to build a health monitoring software that gathers health indicators and enables astronauts to evaluate and act on the status of their health.

## Background
- NASA's Artemis program recently accomplished a crewed lunar flyby, with plans to send humans to Mars.
- **NASA's 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight:**
  1. Space Radiation
  2. Isolation and Confinement
  3. Distance from Earth
  4. Gravity Fields (altered gravity / microgravity)
  5. Hostile Closed Environments
- **Health Risks:** Immune system changes, bone density loss, cardiovascular events, inadequate nutrition, behavioral health conditions.
- **Key Health Indicators:** Blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar (mg/dL), body temperature, daily sleep hours, mood, and ultrasound/visual inputs.

## Objectives & Design Requirements
- Visual tool helping astronauts self-assess health and receive guidance on treatments/improvements.
- Gather user data via keyboard text, mouse clicks, or camera/image uploads.
- Answer key medical questions in plain English (do not assume astronauts are doctors):
  - Which measurements can be performed in space?
  - Why is an exam needed?
  - How do spaceflight hazards affect these indicators?
  - When does a result warrant attention from crew or Earth flight surgeons?
  - What actions prevent a critical condition?
- **Interactive UI:** Clickable human body figure displaying function and health status per body part/system.

## Potential Considerations
- Support different mission environments: spacecraft, space station, rover, or lunar/planetary base.
- Prompt astronauts to take measurements/actions as part of mission routine.
- Gamification system to engage astronauts with daily health tasks.
- Dynamic charts showing indicator changes over time.
- At-a-glance color coding (Green/Yellow/Red).
- Plain-English instructions covering measurements, indicators, and risks.
